new applications of genetics to other things - like was done for genetic algorithms.  in real-life, genes are constantly changing, there are mechanisms that are repairing it, there are genes jumping around, errors, all kinds of crazy things going on.  can this apply to something like AI/ML where instead of static data, you have variations that can help create new adaptations to unknowns?  Can it be used to make autonomy more creative and harder to defeat?  DNA is instructions, ML uses data as data, not instructions, can we encode actions and not data? some of these genetic behaviors enable ability to fight threats and disease and etc, can we apply some of that to fighting threats on a battlespace?   today's genetic algorithms focus on reproduction, these ideas are taking other parts of the properties and behaviors of genetics and trying to find new ways to replicate them in new things - active things like autonomy as an example.
