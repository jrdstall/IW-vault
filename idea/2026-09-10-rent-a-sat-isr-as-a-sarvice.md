Rent-A-Satellite - put up satellites with core capabilities, people can take full control of it like renting a car, they control it's tasking and it's T&C is all self-managed - doesn't need a ground system to keep it healthy, they can use it however they want for as long as they want, and return it when done.  they can upload their own software that conforms to its onboard API for specialized missions.  so, it's a commerical sat, but anyone could "rent"it for their purposes. 

Variation: rather than rent the whole satellite, they rent "envelopes" of comm, power, time, compute, memory. 

Variation: Do people want control of a satellite, or do they want the effect: "i want to know what's happening on this road"  and not have to know anything about how it's done?  some may want either...
