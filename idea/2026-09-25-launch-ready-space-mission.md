Launch-Ready Space Missions - so often our customers wanted missions in a year or less, but we were always bound by encryption hardware or thrusters or something whose parts had 9-12 month lead times.  what if a company pre-bought some of this hardware on the shelf and could supply it immediately?  then expand to full buses and integrated ground software ready for payload integration, etc.
