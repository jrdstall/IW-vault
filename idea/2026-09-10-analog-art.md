Analog Art: Abstract art using eInk paper, on personal device or large walls.  takes inputs from a variety of sources: weather, social media, sounds (happy, sad, angry) news feeds, calendar events, voice activity, seasons, sports, big events, movie releases, feeds into an analog computer that takes thise inputs as "what's on your mind" and produces a very abstract art represenation of what's percolating in your life - based on information theory concepts.  colors and shapes represent emotions and life
