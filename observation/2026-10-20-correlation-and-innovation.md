correlation in data science is like assocation in innovation.  the difference is, in innovation, the brain is wired to find those associations that are sometimes what data sciencd would spit out, but sometimes not, because we don't understand how the brain stores and relates and associates ideas - especially since they aren't stored as normal data or text with very 'digital' formats.  rather, the brain stores feelings and sights and sounds and smells and memories and all kinds of things besides just text.  our data science tries to take data captured from various sources and tries to put it into some simplest format where it can be stored and analyzed.  what can we learn from how the brain associates?  How can we better model this association? do we need to store different data?  do we need to store different metadata about it? do we need different assocation or correlation algorithms?  different variables within the data?
