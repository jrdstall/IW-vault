[Creative Exercise: Cross-Domain 'What If?' (Custom) — What if LEGO ran a aerospace program?]
Massive focus on really reusable hardware components - broken down to lowest level, and then assemble them in new and creative ways to get the job done before anything new is created.  Hardware-first vision and mindset.  Designers go sit with user teams and really understand them - look into how they work, what are their uniquenesses, characteristics, traits.  Training would be boiled down to its simplest but most effective form - words only when necessary but more visual, hands-on.
