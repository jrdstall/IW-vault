AI LLMs are based on patterns of words in written text extracted from the internet.  But our intelligence mashes so many other things, sight, sound, smell, feel, taste.  We don't think that these do, but have you ever smelled something or been taken back to some time or place?  Have you driven down a road and remembered a specific podcast you were listening to the last time you went down that road?  our brain makes associations between not just words, but imprints of all kinds of  sensory input.  Can we build AI to use multi-dimensional input beyond just words?
