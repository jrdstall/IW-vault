[Creative Exercise: Vault Wildcard: IDEA-A01 — 'Kitchen eInk Display'  ✕  [UNIVERSAL JOINT]]
The kitchen eInk display idea is centered around a "universal joint" or in this case interface.  rather than apps that make everything work, the IO device has a basic interface that allows it to accept display content from anyone who discovers it.  So on my phone or computer, i push content to an IO device and it accepts and displays it.  this means you need some common interface at a lower level than apps.  or it would be nice if it was a lower level than apps.  it could be a generic app that provides a discoverable service that others can push content to, but, what if it was more inherent in the device itself, that it was a discoverable device, not an app, that could accept display content.  and it accepts it in a standardized form.
