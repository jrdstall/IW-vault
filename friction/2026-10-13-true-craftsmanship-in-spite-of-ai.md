"True Craftsmanship will emerge from the proliferation of content and the capaity that AI provides."  statement from CES, but I don't think AI will cause this in the way they think it will.  I think craftsmanship will come by those shedding it, forsaking it, and finding joy in creating in a fully human way.  We are getting to the overload point, where there is too much stimulation, too much handing over our humanity and our capacity to AI.  Reasoning and deep thinking are disappearing.  They claim enhanced creativity, but who will be able to create and think in an AI world where you are losing your skills, knowledge and experience and will be expected to think less so AI can make stuff faster?  The only way it will enhance creativity is by not using it.
