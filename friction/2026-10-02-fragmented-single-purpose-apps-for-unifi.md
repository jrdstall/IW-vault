To see where my son's car is requires Bouncie; to see where my wife is requires Google Maps; to review morning cycling distance requires Strava. Each has a separate map display and siloed location info. A user should be able to ask for today's unified geo-data or family locations and render it on a single shared context without juggling three siloed apps.
