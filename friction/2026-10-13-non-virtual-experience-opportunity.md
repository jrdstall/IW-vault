Will there be a market for non-virtual experiences? With a new revolution of AI we are now handing over yet another part of our humanity - our capacity to create, to think, to innovate, to design, to make.  While that saddens me already, how might we develop new ways that people will need and want and desire to use those parts of their capacity - could it be a form of entertainment?  could it be a new way to work?  Where they actually engage those capacities that we handed over to AI?  Can we find ways to let people use those capacities and they would pay to let us help them use them?  How will people want to feel, connect, experience life away from content and from AI?
