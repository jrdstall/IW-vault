When listening to streaming from my phone and I get in the car, the car bluetooth takes over. When I open my tablet and get out headphones to watch a movie, the headphones connect to a sleeping laptop rather than the awake device currently in use. Connections are rigidly tied to devices first and profiles second, rather than profiles and context first.
