We remain trapped in platform, OS, system, and INT stovepipe thinking in defense operations. Systems force users to find data and manually bridge platforms, rather than focusing on the warfighter role, needs, decisions, and situational awareness. Smart data should find the warfighter and carry its own processing rules, rather than requiring pre-configured platforms and heavy application installations.
