We have immense hardware duplication—multiple laptops, tablets, and phones across work and personal contexts. They all have compute, memory, and screens, taking power and resources to build and network, yet are rarely used simultaneously. Because software lacks contextual profile switching with strong isolation, we duplicate physical hardware rather than contextually switching profiles on the same iron.
